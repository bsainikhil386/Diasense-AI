Protocol
From data to decision in three steps.


01
Enter your data
Age, height and weight, glucose, HbA1c, blood pressure, family history, activity, symptoms, earlier results and pregnancy history, all validated as you type.


02
Model scoring
A transparent weighted logistic score using standard screening cut-offs. Readings in the diabetes range trigger a confirm-with-a-doctor alert.


03
Act on it
Get tier-based advice on doctor visits, tests and lifestyle, then download a PDF report with your name, sex and age to share with your clinician.
